# Surface brief: Solveit Indonesia public site (home and inner pages)

Scope: public company profile and portfolio (/, /tentang, /layanan, /portofolio, case studies, /proses, /kontak, 404). Visitor mode: Persuade (Experience on portfolio pages). Audience: Indonesian business owners and decision makers evaluating a software vendor, often on a phone. Primary action: start a consultation (service picker in the hero, contact page composing a WhatsApp or email message). Proof available: real services, process, confirmed contacts; portfolio entries are labelled demonstration concepts. Build path: code-led (no image generation in this environment). Direction pinned by the brief ("Precision in Motion", palette from the logo); concept-seed was not rolled.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is assembled from the logo's own geometry: four capsules on a 33 degree axis that lock into an S. It refuses the SaaS template (centred headline over a gradient, three equal feature cards, testimonial carousel).

OWN-WORLD: Cool porcelain ground (#f1f4f9), logo navy ink (#082a56), logo cobalt (#1452ed) as the single accent and as committed fields (closing CTA band, bento tile, process panels), cyan only inside capsule gradients. Archivo variable with the width axis: semi-expanded heavy display, normal-width text. Full capsules for every single-line control; 28px panels; 33 degrees is the only diagonal (lattice, cover art, menu icon, footer wipe).

STORY: Understand what Solveit builds, believe the engagement is disciplined (written scope, previews, maintenance), see how they think through concept case studies, then start a consultation in the channel they already use.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left seven columns: two to three line display headline, one sentence lede, a working service picker (radio capsules) with the primary "Mulai konsultasi" submit and a "Lihat portofolio" link. Right five columns: the logo mark at about 30rem assembling from its four capsules over a faint capsule lattice, pointer depth on fine pointers. 72px header with the primary lockup.

FORM: Brief-pinned direction, ordered first; no seed key (pinned, not rolled). Signature interaction: the mark assembly; motion grammar: scroll-coupled reveals with distinct roles per section (word brightening, rail fill, diagonal wipe, horizontal pan, sticky stack).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
